Home / Help / Returns

Returns

Unopened items can be returned within 30 days. A runner picks them up from your hall, so there's nothing to print or ship.

  • Refunds go back to your original payment within 3–5 business days of pickup.
  • Opened bedding, towels and shower shoes can't be returned for hygiene reasons unless they arrived damaged.
  • Damaged or wrong item? We'll replace it free, opened or not.

Home / Campus / Office delivery

Office and study room delivery

Stuck in the library, a lab or a campus office? We'll bring supplies to you.

  • Choose Other / Off Campus as your delivery location.
  • At checkout, enter the building and room, like "Library, 3rd floor study room 312".
  • Add a note if the runner needs to ask at a front desk.

Popular for offices

Five Star notebooks, Pilot G2 pens, Post-it flags, Anker charging cables, Clorox wipes and the LED clamp desk lamp.

Home / About / Our story

Our story

Dorm Dash started with a familiar problem: it's move-in day, you forgot the shower caddy, and you don't have a car.

Most students live a long walk or a rideshare away from the nearest big store. We built Dorm Dash so the things you need for your room and your classes come to you, delivered by students who live on the same campus.

We stock what fits a dorm: Twin XL sizes, items that follow housing rules, and kits that cover move-in, finals and sick days in one order.
