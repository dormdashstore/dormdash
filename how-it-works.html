How Dorm Dash works

Order from your phone and a student runner brings it to your door. Here's what happens from cart to doorstep.

Four steps to your door

  1. Choose where to deliverPick your residence hall from the bar at the top of any page. Choose Other / Off Campus to enter an address.
  2. Fill your cartShop bedding, desk supplies, storage and kits. Standard delivery is free once your cart reaches $35.
  3. Pick your speedStandard arrives in 2–4 hours. Dash gets there in under an hour for $5.99.
  4. Meet your runnerWe text you when your runner is at your building. Pay by card, campus card or at the door.

Delivery times and prices

Runners are out 8am to 1am, seven days a week. Orders placed after 1am go out with the first runs at 8am.

Office, library and off-campus delivery

Stuck in the library, a lab or a campus office? We'll bring supplies to you.

  • Choose Other / Off Campus as your delivery location.
  • At checkout, enter the building and room, like "Library, 3rd floor study room 312".
  • Add a note if the runner needs to ask at a front desk.
Large itemsMini fridges and mattress toppers are carried to your door. No elevator? Runners take the stairs at no extra charge.
ReturnsUnopened items can go back within 30 days. Schedule a pickup and a runner collects it.
Not home when it arrives?Your runner texts and waits 5 minutes, then leaves it at your door if your hall allows it.
