About Dorm Dash

Dorm Dash started with a familiar problem: it's move-in day, you forgot the shower caddy, and you don't have a car.

Our story

Most students live a long walk or a rideshare away from the nearest big store. We built Dorm Dash so the things you need for your room and your classes come to you, delivered by students who live on the same campus.

We stock what fits a dorm: Twin XL sizes, items that follow housing rules, and kits that cover move-in, finals and sick days in one order.

Open 8am to 1amSeven days a week, including move-in weekend and finals.
7 residence hallsBell, Foundation, Parkhurst, Napier, Adams, Sanford and Wells, plus off-campus addresses.
