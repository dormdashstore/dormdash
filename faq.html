Ordering and payment

How do I place an order?

Choose your hall at the top of the page, add items to your cart, then tap the cart and check out. You'll get an order number right away.

What payment methods do you take?

Credit or debit card, your campus card, or pay at the door when your runner arrives.

Do I need an account?

No. Signing in just saves your hall and makes checkout faster.

Is there a minimum order?

No minimum. Standard delivery is $3.99 and free once your order reaches $35.

Delivery

Where do you deliver?

Bell, Foundation, Parkhurst, Napier, Adams, Sanford and Wells halls, plus campus offices, the library and nearby off-campus addresses. See all locations.

How fast is delivery?

Standard delivery arrives in 2–4 hours. Dash delivery arrives in under an hour on campus and in about 55 minutes off campus.

What are your hours?

8am to 1am, seven days a week. Orders placed after 1am go out at 8am.

What if I'm not there when it arrives?

Your runner texts you and waits 5 minutes, then leaves the order at your door if your hall allows it. Otherwise we bring it back and redeliver once for free.

Can you bring a mini fridge up the stairs?

Yes. Large items are carried to your door at no extra charge, even without an elevator.

Products

Will the sheets fit my dorm bed?

Almost every dorm bed is Twin XL (39 × 80 in). Standard twin sheets are 5 inches too short. All our bedding is cut for Twin XL.

What does RA-approved mean?

Items tagged RA-approved follow the rules most housing offices enforce, like UL-listed surge protectors instead of extension cords. Read the RA-approved guide.

What comes in the kits?

Each kit lists every item on its product card. See the Move-In, Finals Week and Sick Day kits.

Something I want is out of stock. What now?

We restock weekly. Send us a message and we'll tell you when it's back.

Returns and refunds

How do returns work?

Unopened items can be returned within 30 days. Schedule a pickup and a runner collects it from your door. There's nothing to print or ship.

When will I get my refund?

Refunds go back to your original payment within 3–5 business days after pickup.

What if my item arrived damaged or wrong?

We'll replace it for free, opened or not. Choose "Arrived damaged" or "Wrong item sent" when you schedule the pickup.

Can I return opened bedding or towels?

Opened bedding, towels and shower shoes can't be returned for hygiene reasons unless they arrived damaged.

Working at Dorm Dash

How do I become a runner?

Apply on our About page. You need to be a currently enrolled student.

Do runners keep their tips?

Yes. Runners earn per delivery plus 100% of tips.
